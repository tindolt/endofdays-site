Privacy Policy

End Of Days · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Summary

End Of Days does not sell your data, does not show ads and does not run its own analytics. The game only uses what it needs to let you play online with friends.

What the game uses

  • Platform account info. When you play online through Steam or Epic Online Services, the game receives your platform account ID and display name. These are used to show your name to other players and to connect you to multiplayer sessions.
  • Multiplayer sessions. When you host or join a game, session details (such as the session name, player count and your network address) are shared with the platform's matchmaking service and with the other players in that session so the game can connect you.
  • Local saves and settings. Your settings, progression and saved runs are stored only on your own device.

Third-party services

Online play is provided by Valve (Steam) and Epic Games (Epic Online Services). Their own privacy policies apply to the data they process:

  • Steam Privacy Policy
  • Epic Games Privacy Policy

LAN play does not use any online service.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can play offline or on LAN without signing in to an online service. You can delete your local saves at any time by removing the game's save folder.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
